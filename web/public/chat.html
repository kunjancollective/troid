What it is

A chat over TROID.md, the rules on troid's compare, the methodology and a fixed support script. Sizing, budgets and compliance checks run through tools that do the arithmetic and name the document and read date of every rule they use, or say its source is not yet recorded; the model reports what they return. Every number carries its tier. A pending rule stays pending.

What it isn't

A signal service, a firm recommendation, a memory of you, or a way to reach your account. troid stores nothing you type; the page keeps the conversation only while it is open. To answer, troid sends the conversation to the company that provides the AI model, which may keep it for a limited period under its own terms. troid's log records one line per message: answered or failed, tool calls, the model called, whether the reply was the abuse warning, the fixed reply given when the AI model declines to answer, or the end of the session, and an error's status code. Never the text, never your address. The hosting provider keeps its own request logs, which can include your IP address. About 20 messages an hour per address.
