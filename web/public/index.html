What troid does

Computes both loss ceilings and reports the binding one. Sizes positions net of the 0.04%-per-side fee. Checks trades against firm rules — the ten-day maximum hold, the margin concentration limit, the five-day minimum. Works across every challenge type and every asset the firm lists.
