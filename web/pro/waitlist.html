What troid Pro adds

  • Saved accounts, up to five

    Each keeps its firm, product, balance, day start and high-water mark, so troid's desk opens with the account filled in.

  • Rule-change alerts

    An email when a firm changes a rule that applies to one of your saved accounts: the old text, the new text and the source.

  • Journal check

    Give it a firm's trade-history file and get back every breach and near-miss (within 20% of a limit), the fee drag on each trade, and hold-limit and concentration flags, each finding cited to the firm's rule. The file is read in your browser: your trades never leave your device, and troid stores none of them.

    The same assistant, with more messages a day.
