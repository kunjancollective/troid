## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **10.4 s**

> Every funded account has two separate loss ceilings, and they're measured differently and bind at different times.
> 
> **Daily loss limit** — how much the account can lose in one trading day before the account fails that day.
> ```
> daily_limit = quota × daily_loss%
> daily_budget = daily_limit + (equity − day_start)
> ```
> On Bitfunded, the FAQ says this is a FIXED amount computed from the initial balance (quota), not from the current day's start — so it doesn't grow or shrink with the account's equity curve, only the running day's P&L shifts the remaining room.
> 
> **Maximum loss** — a floor on the whole account, measured from the quota (static, at Bitfunded) or from a trailing high-water mark (at firms like BrightFunded):
> ```
> dd_floor = quota × (1 − max_loss%)        [static]
> dd_budget = equity − dd_floor
> ```
> 
> **Why it matters:** only the smaller of the two — `effective_budget = min(daily_budget, dd_budget)` — is what actually constrains the next trade. The two swap which one binds at a crossover equity level:
> ```
> crossover = quota × (1 − max% + daily%)
> ```
> On a $100,000 Bitfunded 1-Step (daily 4%, max 6%, both SOURCED — Bitfunded help centre, Challenge & Trader Stage, and Terms of Use 9(a), read 2026-09-23; daily basis from the FAQ, read 2026-09-21), that crossover is **$98,000** — only $2,000 below the starting balance. Below $98,000, the 6% maximum-loss floor is what actually binds, and the advertised 4% daily limit isn't the real constraint that day; above $98,000, the daily limit binds.
> 
> What this means for an account: which ceiling matters depends on the day's starting balance, not just on current equity — a trader near $98,000 needs to check both, every day, because the tighter one can switch overnight.
>
