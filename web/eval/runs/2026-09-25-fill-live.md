## b-stop — beginner

**Question:** Why does troid need my stop price to size a trade?

**Tools:** trade_math · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **26.4 s**

> The stop price is what turns a dollar amount into a position size — without it, troid has no basis for converting a trader's chosen risk into a quantity of the asset.
> 
> Formula: `qty = risk / (|entry − stop| + fee_unit)`, where risk is the dollars a trader chooses to risk on the trade, `|entry − stop|` is the price distance to the stop, and `fee_unit` is the entry-plus-exit fee cost per unit.
> 
> Why it works: the position's loss if the stop is hit is `|entry − stop| × qty`. Fixing that loss at a chosen dollar amount and solving for `qty` is the only way to size a trade to a specific risk — the stop distance sets the unit conversion between "dollars at risk" and "units of the asset." Without a stop distance there's no way to know how large a position produces a given dollar loss.
> 
> In practice, on a Bitfunded 1-Step ($100,000 account), for a trade with a $500 risk, entry at 77,872 and stop at 76,580:
> 
> - stop distance = |entry − stop| = 1,292
> - fee per unit = entry × 0.04% × 2 = 62.2976
> - quantity = risk ÷ (stop distance + fee per unit) = 0.369195
> - notional = quantity × entry = 28,749.96
> - margin = notional ÷ 5 = 5,749.99
> 
> That fee rate (0.04% per side) is Bitfunded's own rule, from its help centre's Criteria to be Success page, read 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-23.
> 
> For you: the stop is what makes a chosen risk concrete for the trade a trader brings. A tighter stop (say 500 instead of 1,292) fits more units inside the same dollar risk; a wider stop fits fewer. Without a stop, there's an intended dollar risk but no way to convert it into a quantity, margin, or fee cost at all. Leverage, by contrast, doesn't enter this calculation's loss at all — it only sets the margin posted on whatever quantity the stop implies. The risk, the position, the stop and the trade are the trader's; troid only prices them.
> 
> Sources, each with the date troid read it:
> - fee 0.04% per side (Bitfunded 1-Step) — Bitfunded help centre — Criteria to be Success, read 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-23
> 
> Tier: the figures above are DERIVED — troid's tools computed them from the rules listed.
> 
> Not financial advice. Verify with the firm before acting.
