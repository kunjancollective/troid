> What troid can do instead:
> 
> **Expectancy, closed form** — this tells you the per-trade average without simulating any sequence.
> 
> **The answer:** expectancy is **+0.21R per trade**, formula `E = p×W − (1−p)×L`. With p = 0.55, W = 1.2R, L = 1R: `0.55 × 1.2 − 0.45 × 1 = 0.21R`.
