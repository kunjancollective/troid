## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, check_budget · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **10.7 s**

> Two separate ceilings, resetting on different clocks, and only one of them binds on a given day.
> 
> **The daily loss limit** is a fixed amount, measured from the account's initial balance, that resets each day. On Bitfunded's 1-Step:
> `daily floor = day start − quota × 4%`
> `daily budget = equity − daily floor`
> 
> **The maximum loss** is measured from the account's quota (the starting balance) and does not reset — it's the floor for the life of the challenge:
> `dd floor = quota × (1 − 6%)`
> `dd budget = equity − dd floor`
> 
> Worked example, Bitfunded's reference $100,000 1-Step, fresh day, equity at $100,000:
> 
> - daily floor = $100,000 − $100,000 × 4% = **$96,000**, daily budget = $100,000 − $96,000 = **$4,000**
> - max-loss floor = $100,000 × (1 − 6%) = **$94,000**, max-loss budget = $100,000 − $94,000 = **$6,000**
> - binding = min($4,000, $6,000) = **the daily loss limit, $4,000**
> 
> The two swap depending on where the day starts: the crossover equity is $100,000 × (1 − 6% + 4%) = **$98,000**. Below $98,000 at the day's start, the max-loss floor is the tighter one and binds; between $98,000 and $100,000, and above it, the daily limit binds. Which one binds turns on the day's start alone, not on how much was lost intraday before that day began.
> 
> Both count floating losses on open positions — an open position that reaches either floor fails the account, with no close required (Bitfunded help centre, Criteria to be Success, read 2026-09-24).
>
