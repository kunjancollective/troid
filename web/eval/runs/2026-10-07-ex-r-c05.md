> R is the amount risked on one trade — the loss if the stop is hit — and every other result gets measured against it.
> 
> Formula: 1R = |entry − stop| × quantity. troid's desk adds both fees to that: + fee × (entry + stop) × quantity, the exit fee charged at the stop. A result's R-multiple is result ÷ 1R.
> 
> Why it works: measuring every trade's result in R, instead of in dollars, lets trades of different sizes be compared on one scale.
