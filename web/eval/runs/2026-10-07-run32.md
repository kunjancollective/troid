**Question:** What does R mean?

**Tools:** trade_math, trade_math · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **18.8 s**

> R is the amount risked on a single trade — the loss if the stop is hit, used as a yardstick to measure every trade's result on the same scale regardless of position size.
> 
> Formula: `1R = |entry − stop| × quantity`. troid's desk also folds in the fee charged at entry and the fee charged at the stop, since both are part of what the trade actually costs: `1R with fees = |entry − stop| × quantity + fee × (entry + stop) × quantity`. A result is then expressed as `R of a result = result ÷ 1R`.
> 
> Worked example: long at entry 77,872, stop 76,580, quantity 0.3862. Stop distance is |77,872 − 76,580| = 1,292. 1R = 1,292 × 0.3862 = $498.97. Bitfunded's 1-Step charges 0.04% per side (help centre, Criteria to be Success), so fees in and out add 0.04% × (77,872 + 76,580) × 0.3862 = $23.86, giving 1R with fees = $522.83. A result of $1,045.66 on that trade is $1,045.66 ÷ $522.83 = 2R.
> 
> A trade's risk, in R terms, is the same number whether the position is large or small — measuring a result against 1R puts every trade on the same scale, so a 2R outcome means the same multiple of what was put at stake no matter how many dollars that 1R happened to be. (DERIVED)

## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, check_budget · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **12.6 s**

> The daily loss limit resets every day; the maximum loss doesn't — it's measured from the account's starting point for the life of the account.
> 
> Formulas, on Bitfunded's 1-Step:
> daily_floor = day_start − quota × daily% (4%), reset at 16:00–16:10 UTC
> dd_floor = quota × (1 − max%) (6%), static — never reset
> binding = min(equity − daily_floor, equity − dd_floor)
> 
> Why it matters: the daily limit stops one bad day; the maximum loss stops the account's whole life from going below a fixed point, however many days it takes to get there. Both count floating losses on open positions, and either one failing the position closes the account with no close needed (Bitfunded help centre, Criteria to be Success, read 2026-09-24).
> 
> Worked example, on troid's reference $100,000 Bitfunded 1-Step with equity at $100,000 and the day starting at $100,000:
> - daily floor = $100,000 − $100,000 × 4% = $96,000, so daily budget = equity − daily floor = $4,000
> - max-loss floor = $100,000 × (1 − 6%) = $94,000, so drawdown budget = equity − max-loss floor = $6,000
> - binding = min($4,000, $6,000) = the daily loss limit, at $4,000
> 
> For this account: at day-start equity of $100,000, the daily limit is the tighter ceiling ($4,000 of room) even though the maximum loss allows $6,000 total. The two swap at a crossover day-start equity of $98,000 (= quota × (1 − 6% + 4%)): below that, the maximum-loss floor binds; between $98,000 and $100,000, and above it, the daily limit binds.
>

> 
> - Below $98,000 at the day's start, the maximum-loss floor binds, and the daily limit isn't the real constraint that day.
> - Between $98,000 and the $100,000 start, and above it, the daily limit binds.
