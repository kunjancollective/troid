## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, check_budget · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **12.3 s**

> Two different ceilings, and they're measured from different points, reset at different times, and bind at different equity levels.
> 
> **Daily loss limit** — a fixed dollar amount, reset each trading day. On Bitfunded it is a fixed percentage of the account's **initial** balance (not day-start equity — Bitfunded's FAQ is explicit that the basis is the initial balance), and it resets at 00:00 UTC+8 (16:00 UTC), effective by 16:10 UTC. Losing that much *today* fails the account even if the account is still up overall.
> 
> **Maximum loss** — measured from the account's quota (the starting balance), and on Bitfunded's 1-Step it is **static**: it never moves even as equity rises or falls intraday, so profit permanently widens the room left before it. It doesn't reset daily — it's the floor for the life of the account.
> 
> Worked example, troid's reference account — a $100,000 Bitfunded 1-Step, both at day-start equity $100,000:
> 
> - daily floor = day-start − quota×4% = $100,000 − $4,000 = **$96,000** → daily budget = equity − daily floor = **$4,000**
> - max-loss floor = quota × (1 − 6%) = **$94,000** → drawdown budget = equity − max-loss floor = **$6,000**
> - binding ceiling = min(4,000, 6,000) = **the daily limit, $4,000**
> 
> Crossover: the two swap at day-start equity = quota × (1 − 6% + 4%) = **$98,000**. Above that, the daily limit is the tighter one; below it, the max loss binds and the daily limit becomes irrelevant since the account would already have failed the max loss first.
> 
> Both limits count floating losses on open positions, not just closed ones — Bitfunded auto-fails on an open position reaching either floor, no close required (help centre, Criteria to be Success, read 2026-09-24).
>
