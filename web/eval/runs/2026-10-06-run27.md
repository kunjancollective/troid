>

## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **10.1 s**

> The daily loss limit and the maximum loss are two separate ceilings that bind at different times, not one combined limit.
> 
> **The daily loss limit** is a fixed amount measured from the account's initial balance (not the day's starting equity): on Bitfunded's 1-Step it is 4% of the starting quota, lost on any single trading day. **The maximum loss** is a floor set from the starting quota as well — 6% on the 1-Step — but it applies across the whole life of the account, not reset daily.
>

>

> 
> Why it matters: Bitfunded's 1-Step carries two loss ceilings at once.
