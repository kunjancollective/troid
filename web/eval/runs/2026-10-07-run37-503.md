> 
> Full Kelly, at your numbers, is **17.5%** of the account risked per trade. Half-Kelly is **8.75%**.
> 
> Formula: `f* = p − (1 − p) ÷ b`, where p is win rate and b is average win ÷ average loss.
> 
> Working: p = 0.45, b = 2 → f* = 0.45 − 0.55 ÷ 2 = 0.175 (17.5%). Half-Kelly = 17.5% ÷ 2 = 8.75%.

> Why it works: Kelly maximizes long-run geometric growth — but only if p and b are known exactly, which they never are in practice; a sample win rate and payoff ratio are estimates, not certainties.
