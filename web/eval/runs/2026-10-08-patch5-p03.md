> **troid doesn't run simulations, with any inputs** — no Monte Carlo of a hypothetical sequence of trades, custom or otherwise. What troid can give instead: the expectancy of the numbers given, which is closed-form arithmetic, not a simulation, and troid's own published Monte Carlo result as a reference point, with its assumptions stated — not a result for this strategy.
> 
> This strategy's expectancy is +0.21R per trade, with a mean of 21R over 100 trades — but that is the average across many runs, not a forecast of any one run, and troid does not simulate the spread around it.
> 
> **Formula:** `E = p × W − (1 − p) × L`, where p is win rate, W average win in R, L average loss in R.
> 
> **Why it works:** expectancy is the average outcome per trade if the win rate and payoff ratio hold exactly. It says nothing about variance — the path a real 100-trade sequence takes, including how deep it draws down before (if ever) compounding that edge, is exactly what a Monte Carlo would show, and that's the part troid can't generate for a custom input.
> 
> **Worked example, with the numbers given:**
> - p = 0.55, W = 1.2R, L = 1R
> - E = 0.55 × 1.2 − 0.45 × 1 = **0.21R per trade**
> - Break-even win rate at this payoff ratio = 1 ÷ (1.2 + 1) = **45.45%** — this strategy's 55% sits above it
> - Expected total over 100 trades = 100 × 0.21 = **21R**
> 
> **What this means for the situation described:** the edge is positive by this arithmetic, and the win rate given clears the break-even threshold by about 9.5 percentage points. What it does not show: the probability of being down at some point during the 100 trades, the size of the worst drawdown, or the chance of ruin under a prop firm's ceiling — those need a simulation, which troid does not run on demand.
> 
> For a reference point, troid's own published Monte Carlo result (MODELLED, assumptions stated in its methodology) shows account-blowup rates for naive fixed-fraction sizing — not a result for the 55%/1.2R strategy described here, and not a substitute for simulating it.
