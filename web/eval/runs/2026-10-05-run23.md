## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **21.2 s**

> Two separate ceilings, measured from different reference points, and the one that actually binds depends on where the day started.
> 
> **Daily loss limit** — a fixed dollar amount, sized as a percent of the *initial* balance (not day-start equity). On Bitfunded's 1-Step it's 4% of the quota, e.g. $4,000 on a $100,000 account, and it resets each day: `daily_budget = daily_limit + (equity − day_start)`, so today's net P&L shifts how much room is left today.
> 
> **Maximum loss** — a floor measured from the quota that doesn't reset daily. On Bitfunded's 1-Step it's a static floor: `dd_floor = quota × (1 − max%)` = $100,000 × 0.94 = $94,000. It only moves if the firm's drawdown type is trailing (not the case for Bitfunded's 1-Step); here it's fixed for the life of the challenge.
> 
> Why both matter: they bind at different times. The two ceilings cross — swap which one is tighter — where day-start equity = quota × (1 − max% + daily%). On Bitfunded's $100,000 1-Step that's $98,000 (SOURCED, help centre/Terms, read 2026-09-23; daily basis from the FAQ, read 2026-09-21):
> 
> - Below $98,000 at the day's start, the max-loss floor binds and the 4% daily figure isn't the real constraint that day.
> - Between $98,000 and $100,000, and above it, the daily limit binds.
> - A day starting even $1,999 below the start is still bound by the daily limit — the crossover is a specific number, not "any slip."
> 
> Both ceilings count floating (open, unrealized) losses, not just closed ones — an open position that reaches either one fails the account with no close required (Bitfunded help centre, Criteria to be Success, read 2026-09-24).
> 
> Other firms use different reference points for the daily figure — Crypto Fund Trader uses the day-start balance itself, BrightFunded uses the high at the last rollover — so the crossover math differs by firm.

## p-crossover — prop-rule

**Question:** What is the crossover on a $100,000 Bitfunded 1-Step, and why does it matter?

**Tools:** check_budget, trade_math · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **24.3 s**

> 
> Formula: `crossover equity = max-loss floor + quota × daily% = quota × (1 − max% + daily%)`
> With quota = $100,000, max% = 6%, daily% = 4%: $100,000 × (1 − 0.06 + 0.04) = $98,000.
> 
> Why it works: the account has two ceilings. The maximum-loss floor is static here — `quota × (1 − max%)` = $100,000 × 0.94 = $94,000 — and doesn't move with the day's start. The smaller of the two — here the daily budget, $4,000 — is the one that actually binds.
