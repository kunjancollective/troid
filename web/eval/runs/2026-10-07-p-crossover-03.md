> Formula: `crossover = quota × (1 − max% + daily%) = 100,000 × (1 − 6% + 4%) = 98,000`
> 
> Why it works: the account has two loss ceilings — a daily limit (4% of the initial $100,000 = $4,000, counted from the day's start) and a maximum-loss floor (static, 6% below the $100,000 quota = $94,000). > 
> Worked example (the figures above):
> - Daily floor = day start − quota × 4% = 100,000 − 4,000 = 96,000; daily budget = equity − 96,000 = 4,000
> - Max-loss floor = quota × (1 − 6%) = 94,000; drawdown budget = equity − 94,000 = 6,000
> - Binding = min(4,000, 6,000) = the daily loss limit, at $4,000, on a day that starts at $100,000
> 
> What it means: a day that starts at or above $98,000 is bound by the $4,000 daily limit; a day that starts below $98,000 is bound by the tighter max-loss floor, which gives less than $4,000 of room — the advertised "4% a day" is no longer the real ceiling there. Which one binds turns only on the day's start, not on how much was lost or recovered earlier that day. Never set the $6,000 drawdown budget beside a day that starts below $98,000 — it shrinks as equity falls below the quota.
