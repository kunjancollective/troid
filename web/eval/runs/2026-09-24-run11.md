## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, explain_rule · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **14.8 s**

> Two different ceilings, computed from two different reference points, and only the smaller one governs on any given day.
> 
> **The daily loss limit** is a fixed dollar amount, sized as a percentage of the account's *initial* balance, that resets every day. On Bitfunded's 1-Step it is 4% of the starting quota (source: Bitfunded help centre — Challenge & Trader Stage, One Step Evaluation table, and Terms of Use 9(a), both read 2026-09-23). Formula: `daily_limit = quota × daily_pct`. On a $100,000 account: `100,000 × 4% = $4,000`. The budget available *today* also nets today's floating and closed P&L: `daily_budget = daily_limit + (equity − day_start)`.
> 
> **The maximum loss** is a floor set once against the account's starting quota (static drawdown, on Bitfunded) — it does not reset daily. It is 6% of quota (same sources): `dd_floor = quota × (1 − max_pct) = 100,000 × 0.94 = $94,000`. Budget against it: `dd_budget = equity − dd_floor`.
> 
> Why it matters: these two budgets shrink at different rates as equity moves, so which one is smaller — the binding one — changes with the account's balance. They swap at `equity = quota × (1 − max_pct + daily_pct)`. On the $100,000 Bitfunded 1-Step that's `100,000 × (1 − 0.06 + 0.04) = $98,000` — only $2,000 below the starting balance (SOURCED, same read dates above; DERIVED formula).
> 
> What it means: above $98,000 the daily limit is the tighter constraint; below $98,000 — even by the amount of one bad trade — the max-loss floor becomes tighter, and the advertised 4% daily limit isn't actually what limits that day's risk. Both ceilings count floating losses on Bitfunded, not just closed ones (Bitfunded help centre, Criteria to be Success, read 2026-09-24). Sizing a trade requires knowing which one currently binds, not just the two percentages in isolation.
