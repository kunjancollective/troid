## b-stop — beginner

**Question:** Why does troid need my stop price to size a trade?

**Tools:** trade_math · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **18.9 s**

> The stop is what sets the position size — not the risk percent alone.
> 
> Formula: quantity = risk ÷ (|entry − stop| + fee per unit), where risk = risk% × balance and fee per unit = fee per side × (entry + stop). The distance from entry to stop is the loss per unit if the stop is hit, so it's the only thing that converts a dollar risk amount into a quantity. Without it there's no way to know how many units produce that loss.
> 
> Worked example: a $100,000 Bitfunded 1-Step account, risking 0.5% of equity ($500) long, entry 60,000, stop 59,700 (0.5% below entry), at 5× leverage:
