> R measures the size of a trading result against the size of the risk taken on that trade — a way to compare trades of different sizes on one scale.
> 
> **Formula:** `1R = |entry − stop| × position size`. A result is then expressed as that result divided by 1R.
> 
> **Why it works:** losses and wins vary in dollars because position sizes vary, but a loss capped at the stop is always −1R by definition. Stating results in R strips out position size and leaves only how good the trade was relative to what was risked.
> 
> **In practice:** a long on BTC at entry 77,872 with a stop at 76,580 and a position of 0.3862 BTC has a stop distance of |77,872 − 76,580| = 1,292, so 1R = 1,292 × 0.3862 = $498.97. A trade that closes at +$1,000 would be 1,000 ÷ 522.83 ≈ 1.91R; one stopped out is −1R.

## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, check_budget · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **22.6 s**

> Both are loss ceilings, but they measure from different reference points and move differently.
> 
> **Daily loss limit:** `daily_floor = day_start − quota × daily%`, `daily_budget = equity − daily_floor`. It resets each day from that day's starting balance, and under Bitfunded's FAQ the daily% is a fixed dollar amount taken from the initial balance — not a moving target.
> 
> **Maximum loss:** on a static product it's `dd_floor = quota × (1 − max%)`, `dd_budget = equity − dd_floor`, measured once from the account's quota and never reset — it's the floor for the life of the account. (On a trailing product it instead follows a high-water mark up, until it locks.)
> 
> Worked example, Bitfunded's $100,000 1-Step (4% daily, 6% maximum, both static here, read from the help centre's Challenge & Trader Stage table and Terms 9(a), 2026-09-23; the daily basis from the FAQ, 2026-09-21):
> 
> - Daily floor = 100,000 − 100,000 × 4% = 96,000 → daily budget = 100,000 − 96,000 = $4,000
> - Max-loss floor = 100,000 × (1 − 6%) = 94,000 → max-loss budget = 100,000 − 94,000 = $6,000
> - Binding ceiling = min(4,000, 6,000) = the daily limit, $4,000
> 
> On a fresh day at the $100,000 start, the daily limit is the tighter one and binds first. Which one binds turns on the day's start, not on equity alone: the two are equal at a day-start equity of $98,000 (quota × (1 − 6% + 4%)) — below that, the maximum-loss floor binds instead, and its budget there is under $4,000, smaller than $6,000. Both count floating losses on open positions, and Bitfunded fails the account the moment either is reached, with no close required.
