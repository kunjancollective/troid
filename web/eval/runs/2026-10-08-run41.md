## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, check_budget · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **9.3 s**

> The daily limit resets every trading day and is sized off the initial balance; the maximum loss is a single floor for the whole account, sized off the starting quota — whichever leaves less room on a given day is the one that actually binds.
> 
> Formulas (Bitfunded 1-Step):
> `daily_floor = day_start − quota × daily%` → `daily_budget = equity − daily_floor`
> `dd_floor = quota × (1 − max%)` → `dd_budget = equity − dd_floor`
> binding ceiling = whichever budget is smaller.
> 
> Worked example, troid's reference $100,000 1-Step, day start = equity = $100,000: daily floor = 100,000 − 100,000×4% = $96,000, daily budget = 100,000 − 96,000 = $4,000. Max-loss floor = 100,000×(1−6%) = $94,000, drawdown budget = 100,000 − 94,000 = $6,000. The smaller of the two, $4,000, is the binding one — here, the daily limit.
> 
> The crossover where the two swap is `quota × (1 − max% + daily%) = $98,000`: a day starting below $98,000 is bound by the $94,000 max-loss floor instead, with under $4,000 of room that day; a day starting at or above $98,000 (including at the $100,000 start) is bound by the daily limit.
