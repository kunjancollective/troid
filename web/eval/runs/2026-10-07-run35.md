> R is a way of measuring a trade's result as a multiple of what was risked on it, rather than in dollars.
> 
> Formula: 1R = |entry − stop| × position size (the loss if the stop is hit); a result is then reported as result ÷ 1R.
> 
> Why it matters: a dollar loss means something different on a $10,000 account than on a $100,000 one. Measuring in R puts every trade, on every account size, on the same scale, so wins and losses can be compared and averaged.
> 
> In practice: a long on BTC at entry 77,872 with a stop at 76,580, size 0.3862 BTC. The stop sits 1,292 below entry (|77,872 − 76,580|), so 1R = 1,292 × 0.3862 = 498.97. A result of 1,000 on this trade works out to 1,000 ÷ 522.83 = 1.913R; a stop-out is −1R.
>

## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, check_budget · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **12.6 s**

> The daily loss limit resets each trading day; the maximum loss is set once, from the account's starting balance, and never resets.
> 
> **Daily loss limit:** `daily_floor = day_start − quota × daily%`, and the budget left against it is `daily_budget = equity − daily_floor`. On Bitfunded this is a fixed dollar amount measured from the initial balance, not the day's own starting equity (Bitfunded FAQ).
> 
> **Maximum loss:** `dd_floor = quota × (1 − max%)` (static, on Bitfunded), and `dd_budget = equity − dd_floor`. It doesn't reset daily — it's one floor for the life of the account, below the quota.
> 
> Why the distinction matters: they bind at different times depending on where the day starts, and troid always sizes against the smaller of the two. On troid's reference account, a $100,000 Bitfunded 1-Step (daily 4%, max 6% static, help centre/Terms, read 2026-09-23), starting a fresh day at equity $100,000:
> 
> - daily floor = $100,000 − $100,000×4% = $96,000 → daily budget = $4,000
> - max-loss floor = $100,000×(1−6%) = $94,000 → max-loss budget = $6,000
> - binding: the daily loss limit, since $4,000 < $6,000
> 
> The two swap at a crossover day-start equity of $98,000 (quota×(1−6%+4%)): below $98,000 the max-loss floor binds and the 4% daily figure isn't the real constraint that day; between $98,000 and $100,000, and above it, the daily limit binds. Both count floating losses on open positions — either one failing the account with no close required (Bitfunded help centre, Criteria to be Success, read 2026-09-24).
> 
> For a $100,000 account on a day that starts at equity, the room against the binding ceiling is $4,000, less than the $6,000 the maximum-loss figure alone would suggest.
