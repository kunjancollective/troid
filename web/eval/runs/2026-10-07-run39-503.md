## b-limits — beginner

**Question:** What's the difference between the daily loss limit and the maximum loss?

**Tools:** explain_rule, check_budget · **model:** claude-sonnet-5 · **13.8 s**

> The short answer: the daily loss limit resets every trading day and is measured from a fixed point; the maximum loss limit doesn't reset — it's the total drawdown ceiling for the life of the account (or stage). They bind at different times, and sizing against the wrong one is a common way accounts fail.
> 
> ## Daily loss limit vs. maximum loss
> 
> **Daily loss limit** — a ceiling on how much equity can fall *within one trading day*, measured from a reference point that resets each day. On Bitfunded, that reference is the account's **initial balance** (not the day's starting equity), so the daily limit is a fixed dollar amount every day — SOURCED to Bitfunded's FAQ, read 2026‑09‑21.
> 
> **Maximum loss** — a ceiling on total drawdown over the account's life (or the stage's), that does not reset daily. Bitfunded's is **static**: measured from the account quota and never recomputed off a high-water mark, so profit permanently widens the room left under it — SOURCED to Bitfunded's help centre, Criteria to be Success, read 2026‑09‑18. (Other firms differ: BrightFunded and Crypto Fund Trader's 1‑Phase trail a high-water mark instead — see below.)
> 
> **Worked example** — Bitfunded $100,000 1‑Step, fresh account, equity = day‑start = $100,000:
> 
> ```
> daily floor  = day_start − quota×4%  = 100,000 − 4,000 = 96,000   → daily budget = 4,000
> dd floor     = quota×(1−6%)          = 94,000                     → dd budget   = 6,000
> binding      = min(4,000, 6,000) = daily loss limit, room = $4,000
> ```
> 
> Both ceilings are SOURCED to Bitfunded's help centre (Challenge & Trader Stage) and Terms 9(a), read 2026‑09‑23, for the 4%/6% sizes.
> 
> **What it means:** near the start of a fresh day with equity at or above $100,000, the *daily* limit is the one that actually binds (smaller room, $4,000 vs $6,000) — the maximum loss only becomes binding once equity has already dropped enough that less room is left under it than under the daily reset. Bitfunded's crossover point for this account is **$98,000** day‑start equity (DERIVED: `quota × (1 − 6% + 4%)`); below that, the maximum loss governs, above it, the daily limit does.
