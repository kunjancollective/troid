# troid logo — final

Mark: a signal dot above its floor. The dot is the status light — the droid is on. The
floor is the loss ceiling. The dot stays above the line; that is the product.

Wordmark: troid in IBM Plex Mono SemiBold with the o replaced by the dot. The dot is
nudged 5% of a cell to the right of geometric centre — an optical correction, because
the r carries its weight on the right and reads closer than it is.
